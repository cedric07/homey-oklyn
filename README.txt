Integrate your Oklyn pool controller with Homey. Monitor water quality and filtration, control the pump and auxiliaries, and get alerts when something needs attention.

In Flow you can react to filtration start/stop, water-quality status changes, and the usual Homey triggers for pH, ORP, salt, water/air temperature and alerts. Conditions and actions cover filtration mode, auxiliaries and measure thresholds.

To get started, create an API key in the Oklyn app under My Account, paste it in the Homey app settings, then add your Oklyn controller.
